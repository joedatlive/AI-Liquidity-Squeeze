# AI-Liquidity-Squeeze
This model investigates a fundamental risk in the rapid adoption of AI: the Liquidity Lock. It simulates the race between Cost-Push Deflation (AI making goods cheaper) and Income Collapse (Labor losing the ability to pay for those goods).
Operating on a differential Marginal Propensity to Consume (MPC), the simulation tracks the Liquidity Squeeze as the Wage Bill is redirected into the Capital Pool.  The model tests the Stabilizer Hypothesis: whether physical infrastructure—the Utility Stabilizers—can serve as a final labor sink to maintain aggregate demand when the Displaced Pool reaches a Critical Threshold of Displacement.

- Initial Population: 330,000,000.
- Annual Labor Displacement Rate: 8%.
- AI Cost Reduction Multiplier: 0.2.
- Utility Labor Absorption Ratio: 0.12 (The rate at which the "Physical Floor" hires the Displaced Pool).
- Subsistence Floor: Defined by the $15,000/year poverty line.
